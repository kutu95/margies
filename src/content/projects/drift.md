Drift selects an image in response to where the viewer stands relative to the screen. Moving through the space becomes a way to explore a photographic collection.

## Your position changes the picture

At the Georgette exhibition, Drift responded to visitors’ movement by selecting photographs. The interaction gives people a physical way to browse images, one at a time.

## Work with your own photographs

A Drift installation can use an image collection supplied by your institution or developed for the project. Image permissions, display requirements and the interaction space are considered as part of the scope.

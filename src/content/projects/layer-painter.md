Visitors contribute a layer over the life of the show. The shared image is the engagement while the exhibition is open, and the object when it closes.

At Georgette the picture grew from what people added. It is not a comment wall. It becomes one artwork.

The same structure can collect a picture for another exhibition, from that exhibition’s own visitors.

A custom web application for recording a veterinary ultrasound study and writing the report. Structured entry, measurements, images, and a finished document.

It is internal software, not a public product. I have a veterinary background; that is why the workflow is familiar. This page is about the software, not clinical services.

Off-the-shelf tools were either too broad or too rigid for the way the exam is actually done.

A visitor stands in front of Captain John Godfrey and talks to him. He answers, aloud, as a person in the room, from the record of a life about 150 years ago.

He is a historic metahuman, made for the Georgette 150th exhibition in Margaret River. The exhibition is over. People used him. [Another can be made the same way](/godfrey).

## What a visitor does

They walk up. They ask something. He replies, with a face and a voice. The conversation is the work.

## At Georgette

Godfrey stood in the exhibition for the 150th anniversary of the SS Georgette, during Margaret River Region Open Studios, 12–27 September 2026. He is one of four works installed together. The others are [Cuborama](/work/cuborama), [Drift](/work/drift) and [Layer Painter](/work/layer-painter).

The [Georgette 150th](/georgette) is the exhibition as a whole.

## How he is made

The record, a face, a voice, and a conversation in the room. Unreal Engine and MetaHuman are how that person is made.

The facts he can talk about are the ones I have been able to establish for the ship and his life. Some of the face is captured, some of it is constructed. When that is wrong, it shows.

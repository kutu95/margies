## How this usually starts

Send a plain description of the problem. What you have now, what you wish happened, and any constraints that matter — budget, location, existing systems, deadlines. I don’t publish fixed prices here because the work varies too much. If it looks like a fit, we talk. If it doesn’t, I’ll say so.

Examples of finished work are on the [projects](/projects) pages and in the [photography](/photography) collections, including the [Georgette exhibition](/projects/ss-georgette-150th), [Metal My Mini](/projects/metal-my-mini), and [aquafarm monitoring](/projects/aquafarm-technology). I do not offer wedding, portrait or event photography as a service.

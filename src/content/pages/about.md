Margies is the name I work under. I live near Margaret River, on a family farm. I used to work as a veterinarian.

I make installations for museums, galleries and places with a story the public should be able to enter. The [Georgette 150th](/georgette) was the first time that work was in a room with visitors: [Captain Godfrey](/work/captain-godfrey), [Cuborama](/work/cuborama), [Drift](/work/drift) and [Layer Painter](/work/layer-painter).

I also take photographs. [Prints](/prints) from the exhibition are available.

Software, electronics and the farm are [other work](/projects). [Metal My Mini](https://metal.margies.app) is a separate workshop, with my son.

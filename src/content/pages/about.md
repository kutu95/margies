I’m based in the Margaret River region of Western Australia. My practice combines artistic ideas with the software and physical systems needed to make them work. I’m interested in experiences where people can ask a question, move through an image or take part in making something.

## Building the experience

My work spans conversational historical characters, immersive projection, responsive photography and tools for painting. I develop both the creative concept and the technology, bringing in specialist contributions where a project needs them.

Captain Godfrey brought together historical research, character development, voice and performance, software and a public installation. The September 2026 exhibition provided an opportunity to observe the conversations people chose to have with him.

## The Georgette project

Photographing the wreck of the SS Georgette and its surrounding coast led into a wider exhibition combining photography, history and interactive work. The Georgette 150th exhibition was presented during Margaret River Region Open Studios in September 2026.

## Working together

If you have a collection, a historical subject or an exhibition idea, I’d be interested to hear about it. We can discuss the experience you would like to create and what developing it might involve.

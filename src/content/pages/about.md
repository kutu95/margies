I live near Margaret River, on a family farm. I used to work as a veterinarian.

I make interactive exhibition and art pieces for galleries, museums and exhibitions. The [Georgette 150th](/georgette) was the first time that work was in a room with visitors: [Captain Godfrey](/godfrey), [Cuborama](/work/cuborama), [Drift](/work/drift) and [Layer Painter](/work/layer-painter).

[Prints](https://exhibition.margies.app/shop) from the exhibition are for sale.

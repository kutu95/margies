## I wish there was something that could just…

A surprising number of useful projects start as an unfinished sentence. Someone has a workflow that almost works, a place that needs watching, an exhibition that needs a room to behave in a particular way, or a process that is being held together with spreadsheets and memory.

The service is essentially this: take that unfinished sentence, work out what “just” would have to do, and determine whether a practical solution can be built with software, electronics, or both.

Sometimes the answer is a small web application. Sometimes it is a sensor and an alert. Sometimes it is a prototype that proves the idea before anyone spends serious money. Occasionally the honest answer is that an existing tool is good enough, and you should use that instead.

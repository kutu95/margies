The property has been a group settlement block, a dairy, an organic winery and now a shared family farm. I run an aquaponics greenhouse there. The photographs that belong with that work — the house of plants and fish, the paddocks, the people doing ordinary jobs — will live in this collection.

The monitoring systems built for the greenhouse are on the [aquafarm technology](/projects/aquafarm-technology) project page. This page is for the pictures, once they are in place.

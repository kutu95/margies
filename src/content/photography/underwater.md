Underwater work is part fieldwork and part waiting. The useful picture is often the one that depends on visibility, swell and a short window when the sand has not covered what you came to see.

Some of these photographs belong with the [SS Georgette collection](/photography/ss-georgette). This page is for the wider underwater work — wrecks, people in the water, and the ground under the coast.
